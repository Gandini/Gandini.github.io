Beedoku privacy policy

Beedoku: Buzzy Logic, by Diniworks. Last updated 21 November 2026.

In short: Beedoku has no accounts and we run no servers. Your progress stays on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob and sends crash reports to Google Firebase. Those Google services collect some device data, described below. We never sell your data.

1. Who we are

Beedoku (Android package com.diniworks.beedoku) is made by Diniworks, an independent developer based in Portugal. Under data protection law (the GDPR) we are the "controller" for the data the services in our app collect on our behalf.

Contact: business.diniworks@gmail.com

2. What stays on your phone

Your game progress and settings (stars, best times, the stage you're playing, hints, Daily Nectar results, statistics, your bee collection and your settings) are stored only on your phone. We don't receive them. You don't need an account.

  • Android's own backup may include this data in your personal Google backup. That is an Android feature you control in your phone's settings.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

3. What is collected, by whom, and why

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob collects your device's advertising ID and app set ID, your IP address (which gives an approximate location), how you interact with ads and the app (for example launches, taps and video views), and diagnostic data (such as launch time and app performance). Where the law requires it, ads are personalised only if you agree. Otherwise you see non-personalised ads. More: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your consent choices (Google User Messaging Platform)

Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent to personalised ads when it starts. Your choice is stored on your phone. You can change it at any time in Settings > Privacy choices (that button appears where the law requires it). To work out which rules apply, the service uses your IP address.

Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report the next time you open it: what went wrong in the code, the state of the app at the time, your device model and Android version, and a random installation ID that doesn't identify you. We use this only to find and fix bugs. Crash reports are kept for 90 days. More: Privacy and security in Firebase.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

If you buy something in the app, Google Play handles the payment. We never see your payment details. The app only learns whether you own the item, so it can unlock it (and restore it if you reinstall). More: Google Privacy Policy.

What we don't use

No sign-in, no location permission, no contacts, camera, microphone or photos, and no analytics beyond the services above.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Consent: personalised ads and the use of ad identifiers where consent is required.
  • Legitimate interests: crash reports (keeping the app working), fraud prevention and non-personalised ads (funding a free game).
  • Contract: delivering an in-app purchase you made.

5. Sharing

Data is shared with Google as our ad network (Google AdMob) and as our service provider for crash reports (Google Firebase). We don't sell your data for money.

US residents: under some US state laws (such as California's), sharing data with an ad network for personalised ads counts as "sharing" or "selling" for targeted advertising. You can opt out with Settings > Privacy choices in the app, where it appears, and by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads, depending on the phone).

6. International transfers

Google processes data in the United States and other countries. Google protects these transfers with safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

7. How long data is kept

  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Ad data: as described in Google's policies (linked above).
  • Data on your phone: until you uninstall the app.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to object to its use, to withdraw your consent at any time, and to complain to a data protection authority (in Portugal, the CNPD; in the UK, the ICO). US state laws give similar rights, including opting out of targeted advertising.

We don't hold data that identifies you directly, so the quickest controls are: Settings > Privacy choices in the app, resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your phone's settings, and uninstalling the app. For anything else, email us at business.diniworks@gmail.com.

9. Children

Beedoku is meant for players aged 13 and over and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you think a child's data has been collected, contact us and we'll help remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
